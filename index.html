Do I have to come with a friend?

No. In fact, coming alone is encouraged. That is the whole point.

No. It is a social community focused on friendship, conversation, and experiences.

Do I need to be outgoing?

Not at all. The activities are designed to make meeting people easier, even if you are shy.
